# Free sound effects for a children's educational game

**All 12 sounds you need can be sourced for free from Freesound.org, Pixabay, Kenney.nl, and Mixkit — with 10 of 12 available under CC0 or no-attribution licenses.** The recommendations below prioritize CC0 (public domain) and the Pixabay Content License, both of which require zero attribution and permit personal/non-commercial use without restriction. Most clips are longer than 0.5 seconds at source and will need a quick trim in Audacity or a similar editor, but all are clean recordings suitable for a toddler game.

## Practical tips for your workflow

**Kenney.nl is the single best bulk source for UI sounds.** Three packs — [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio) (50 sounds), [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) (100 sounds), and [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) (130 sounds) — are entirely CC0 and will cover snd_pop, snd_success, snd_drop, snd_unlock, and potentially snd_whoosh in a single download. This reduces the need to hunt for individual files across multiple sites.

**Trimming is essential.** Most source recordings run 1–25 seconds. Use Audacity (free) to trim each to your target **0.1–0.5 sec** window, normalize volume levels across all 12 clips, and export as 16-bit WAV at 44.1kHz for Android compatibility. For the confetti sound specifically, consider layering the Breviceps pop with a sparkle shimmer trim — Audacity's mix-and-render function handles this in seconds.

**Account requirements differ across sites.** Freesound.org requires a free account to download (but all recommended sounds there are CC0, so no attribution strings attached). Pixabay and Mixkit allow direct downloads without registration. Kenney.nl allows direct download of full packs with no account needed. Zapsplat requires a free account and attribution on the free tier.

**Although your use is non-commercial, every primary recommendation above also clears commercial use** — so if the game ever moves to a published or monetized release, you won't need to re-source your audio. CC0 and the Pixabay Content License both permit unrestricted commercial use without attribution.
